A recent survey discovered that employees who take a "video gaming" break during the day (in other words, they did something that engaged the brain, eyes, hands, and heart) - were 38% happier and more productive. It also stands to reason that type of break is a lot healthier than smoking, and gorging on unhealthy vending food. Who knew video arcades games are not only good for the home, but the office? Which means now, its not "if" you *can* work *and* play. It's you *should* work and play. Just think how cool it would be if every company appreciated this fact, and that every company could use interactive kiosks that gave you the ability to work, learn and play? And the really progressive companies might even put personalized arcade cabinets in their break-rooms. Business and pleasure does mix; like warm cookies and cold milk.
