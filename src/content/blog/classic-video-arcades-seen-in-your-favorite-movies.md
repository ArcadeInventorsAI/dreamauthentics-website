Do you remember some of the greatest Video Arcade Cabinets that were played by your favorite movie star in your favorite movie?

Mame cabinets like [DreamAuthentics](https://dreamauthentics.com/) which are Interactive Arcades that play tens of thousands of your favorite games can help you re-live your favorite old-school games like Galaga and Tron as seen in movies such as War Games, Tron and others. Here is a quick list of some of our favorite movies that feature video games or video arcades.

We wanted to highlight our "Top 2" favorite games with video games featured in them. In the case of the legendary TRON movie it actually spawned many great real-world video games...even though the movie was based on the characters inside a video game.

We hope you enjoy this list and sentimental memories they may bring back.
