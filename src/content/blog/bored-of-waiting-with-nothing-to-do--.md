Sitting at the car dealership (or other places that you have to sit and wait excruciatingly only times) waiting for your car to get serviced can be a long, boring, frustrating ordeal. If only waiting rooms could put in arcade cabinets so that their customers can have something to do. Granted, video arcades aren't what you see everyday or what most people play these days with [PSP](http://www.psp.com) and [xBox](http://www.xbox.com), etc. - but all the more reason for them to be in waiting rooms. They are fun, nostalgic and old-school cool. And if I'm not mistaken - aren't the parents paying the bills? So shouldn't the parents play old school Mame cabinets. So the next time you wait in a waiting room without an arcade - tell them to check out [DreamAuthentics](https://dreamauthentics.com/).
