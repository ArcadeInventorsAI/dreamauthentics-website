We love our customers and they love our Video Arcades! One of our good customers recenly [posted a blog](http://woody.typepad.com/my_weblog/2009/01/my-man-cave.html) showing his AWESOME game room which features one of our Mame Cabinets by [DreamAuthentics](https://dreamauthentics.com/).

As he writes...
THE ARCADE GAME - This is my MAME cabinet custom built for me by [Dream Authentics](https://dreamauthentics.com/). It's a full size arcade machine packed with over 70,00 games. Yes, that's right **70,000**! I have nearly every arcade game, every [Nintendo](http://www.nintendo.com) game, every [Super Nintendo game](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Nintendo_Entertainment_System), every [Nintendo 64 game](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_64), every [Gamecube game](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_GameCube), every [Sega Genesis game](http://strategywiki.org/wiki/Sega_Genesis), every Atari game etc. etc. Never in my life will I play them all, but if you were over and remembered one from the old days we'd be able to play it within minutes. That's all kinds of awesome when you're having a party. My favorites right now are [Joust](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joust_(video_game)), [Gyruss](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyruss), [Tempest](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempest_(arcade_game)), and [Galaga](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galaga).

Thanks for sharing your great looking Game Room and we look forward to posting more of our customer game rooms in our Blog. GAME ON!!
