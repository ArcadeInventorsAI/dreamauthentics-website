Lots of people love to play [classic video arcade games](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_game) on Mame Cabinets like DreamAuthentics.

The term ["MAME" means Multiple Arcade Machine Emulator](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MAME) and is one of the most powerful [emulator](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emulator) technologies to preserve the old-school classic gaming platforms that are no longer being developed or manufactured.

Video Arcades, or Interactive Kiosks as they are called today formerly were single board [PCB (Printed Circuit Boards)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Printed_circuit_board) that did a single task - such as running a video game like [Centipede](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centipede_(video_game)).

Here is a picture of a Centipede [PCB](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Printed_circuit_board) taken out of an 80's Video Arcade cabinet. Today, this entire board is now a piece of software that runs with [MAME](http://mamedev.org/about.html) called a [GAME ROM](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ROM_image) and is purely software.

Customers all over the world love to collect and play many of the old-school (and new school) games on [DreamAuthentics Arcade cabinets](https://dreamauthentics.com/product_page_2_ex.html) because they are fun, easy to use and bring back the enjoyment of the days of the Classic Video Arcades.
