Recently, DreamAuthentics was a co-sponsor at the [Great American Fantasy](http://www.hollywood.com/content/photo_detail.aspx?id=5306884&p=2&dir=next) at the [Playboy Mansion](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Playboy_Mansion).

The evening was highlighted by our presentation of our DreamAuthentics Video Arcade Excalibur to [Mr. Hugh Hefner](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Hefner) (aka Hef) and ["The Girls Next Door"](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girls_Next_Door). Heff told me great stories from the 70's and 80's about how he loved to play arcade games like [Pac Man](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pac-Man), [Centipede](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centipede_(video_game)), and [Missile Command](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missile_Command) in his own personal arcade. When he found out our game cabinet comes with over 150 licensed arcade games including his favorites he really got excited (see picture below)

We created a special ["Girls Next Door"](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Girls_Next_Door) video arcade cabinet(seen below) that is now on permanent display at the mansion. Let us know next time you are in the neighborhood and you can go check out the game at the mansion!
