When you are planning to purchase your ultimate [Video Arcade Cabinet](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_cabinet) that plays [MAME](http://www.mamedev.org/) and thousands of other games the [video arcade monitor display](http://wiki.arcadecontrols.com/wiki/Arcade_Monitors) is nearly the most important aspect you need to understand.

Companies that make Mame cabinets such as [DreamAuthentics](https://dreamauthentics.com/) only uses cutting-edge ARCADE MONITOR DECHNOLOGY.

 Many customers buying new Arcade Cabinets for their home or office make the mistake of buying inexpensive Televisions or inadequate computer [VGA monitors](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SVGA) because of lower prices. In the long run they regret the decision.

One VERY IMPORTANT consideration when buying a Video Arcade cabinet with a classic arcade monitor is the original arcade game's [refresh rate](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refresh_rate), picture quality, color and [aspect ratio](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspect_ratio_%28image%29) of the original game they are playing.

Check out a screen shot of [Ms. Pac Man](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ms._Pac-Man) running wonderfully on a new Arcade Monitor with original aspect ratio and refresh rate!
